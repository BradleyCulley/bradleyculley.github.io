The semester at Colby

-I didn't add very much. I mostly subtracted things.
I made a relatively small number of close friends, while making many more acquantainces.
-talk about spending one night sleeping in the woods, after I was given a roommate
-talk about the flyer that talked about respect with roommates.
For some reason, unlike many times in my life, I took the advice and made a strong effort to be respectful to him.
It worked out. We didn't have any issues, and at the end of the semester we parted ways amicably.
We were friends on Facebook and I think still are. He seemed like a great guy.
Went off and became a mechanical engineer, as I recall.
I could tell he'd had a genuinely good experience with me, which mattered quite a bit to me.
-I read all the reading (books, papers, etc.), cover to cover, for all my classes that had assigned reading, as soon as possible.
The classes in question were Korean Art History and a class about East Asian Religion (TODO: double-check the title/content of that class).

It felt like longer. It was a bit of a slog, even though I was quite interested in the material.
