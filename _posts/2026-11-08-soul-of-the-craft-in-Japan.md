<p>
Japanese cheesemakers in Hokkaido, such as "Niseko Cheese Factory", consistently rank among the best in the world.
Japanese pizzas are also ranked among the best in the world.
Tokyo's "The Pizza Bar on 38th" ranked 2nd globally by one ranking in 2025<sup>1</sup>, and "Pizza Marumo", also located in Tokyo, was ranked 3rd globally by another ranking in 2025<sup>4</sup>.
</p>

<p>
The core technology that will probably cure aging, four genes known as the "Yamanaka factors", was discovered by Dr. Shinya Yamanaka and Dr. Kazutoshi Takahashi at Kyoto University in Kyoto, Japan<sup>2</sup>.
It's the technology behind the first-ever FDA-cleared human clinical trial of age-reversal technology, which started in 2026<sup>3</sup> and is showing promising results so far.
And so on for physics, mathematics, literature, and more.
</p>

<p>
The Japanese idea of Shokunin Kishitsu (職人気質)—"craftsman spirit"—might be at least part of the reason<sup>5</sup>.
Dedicating one's life to honing a craft, no matter how good you get.
The honing itself becomes a goal and source of satisfaction; a fulfillment of the soul through fulfillment of human potential.
What an awesome idea.
</p>
