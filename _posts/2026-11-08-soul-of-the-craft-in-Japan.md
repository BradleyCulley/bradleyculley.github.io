<hr/>

<p>
Japanese cheesemakers in Hokkaido, such as "Niseko Cheese Factory", consistently rank among the best in the world<sup>8,9</sup>.
Japanese pizzerias are also consistently ranked among the best in the world.
Tokyo's "The Pizza Bar on 38th" was ranked 2nd globally according to one ranking in 2025<sup>1</sup>, and "Pizza Marumo", also in Tokyo, was ranked 3rd globally by another ranking the same year<sup>4</sup>.
</p>

<p>
The core technology that will probably cure aging, four genes known as the "Yamanaka factors", was discovered by Dr. Shinya Yamanaka and Dr. Kazutoshi Takahashi at Kyoto University in Kyoto, Japan<sup>2</sup>.
It's the technology behind the first-ever FDA-cleared human clinical trial of age-reversal technology, which started in 2026<sup>3</sup>.
There are many other examples: in physics, mathematics, literature, and more.
</p>

<p>
The Japanese idea of Shokunin Kishitsu (職人気質)—"craftsman spirit"—might be at least part of the reason<sup>5</sup>.
Dedicating one's life to honing a craft, no matter how good you get.
The honing itself kind of becomes the goal; a fulfillment of the soul through fulfillment of one's potential.
</p>
