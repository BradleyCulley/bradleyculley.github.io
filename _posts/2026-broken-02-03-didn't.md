<ul>
    <li>Religious heresy in ancient times didn't cause plagues, natural disasters, or the end of the world</li>
    <li>Communism didn't fix things for poor people</li>    
    <li>Communist governments never quite magically transitioned to a "classless society"⁴-in which the government "is expected to “wither away.”"⁴-after the revolutionary class struggle was sorted out</li>    
    <li>World War I wasn't the war to end all wars</li>
    <li>The Cold War didn't end in an all-out nuclear armageddon, or even nuclear war at all, or any war at all</li>
    <li>Neither moveable type, reading books and magazines, radio, women's suffrage, films, television, women wearing whatever style of clothes they preferred, rock and roll music, cell phones, nor the internet resulted in societal breakdown. Honorable for the internet getting pretty close, though.</li>
    <li>Vietnam becoming communist didn't result in the entire region turning communist, as President Dwight D. Eisenhower's so-called "Domino theory" claimed it would</li>
    <li>Overpopulation didn't destroy the world, leading to horrible famines, as Profession Paul Erlich predicted in his 1968 book titled <i>The Population Bomb</i></li> --TODO add citation
    <li>Violent video games didn't cause violent real-world behavior, despite numerous claims to the contrary⁵. Two separate United States congressional hearings, in 1993 and 1994, were held under the premise that they do cause real-world violence⁶. The inquiry was kicked off after the son of Senator Joe Lieberman's chief of staff asked to get the game <i>Mortal Kombat</i>⁶. "Studies have shown no connection between video games and violent behavior."⁵</li> --TODO
    <li>Japan didn't become the permanent #1 world economic superpower (it's a cool country though).</li>
    <li>The world didn't run out of copper around the year 2000, as Harrison Brown, a scientist at the National Academy of Sciences, predicted it would</li> --TODO add citation
    <li>Y2K didn't crash computer systems all over the world. It didn't do much of anything. Computer programmers just updated almost all the computers beforehand so everything was fine.</li>
    <li>Killer bees didn't kill everyone</li>
    <li>A lack of "Net neutrality" (remember that?) didn't torpedo an openly accessible internet</li> --TODO
    <li>Neither television, films, social media, the internet, nor certain genres of music have made people dumber or increased crime. IQ has been increasing ("the Flynn effect"), and crime decreasing, steadily, for years (TODO: decades?).</li> --TODO add citations
    <li>ISIS didn't take over the Middle East, or even Iraq and Syria as their name aspirationally implies</li>
    <li>Artificial intelligence didn't take everyone's jobs within 6 months of the release of ChatGPT. Self-driving cars replaced neither truck drivers nor taxi drivers. ChatGPT didn't take everyone's jobs over many subsequent 6-month windows since then. ChatGPT didn't replace the need for computer programmers, nor radiologists, as notable public intellectuals have predicted. "PhD-level" models can't accurately spell the word "blueberry".</li>
