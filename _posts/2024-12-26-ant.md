### Ant



<div style="text-align: center">
<br/><i>A poem I wrote in my final semester of high school.<br/>
I didn't like the original title, so I changed it to "Ant" here.
It's what's called a "concrete poem", in that the physical shape of the text is meaningful.</i><br/><br/>
<b style="font-size: 20px;">Ant</b><br/><br/>
             An<br/>
       ant trips over<br/>      
   pieces of dust, stumbles<br/>
her way to an anthill, carries<br/>
  big leaves on her back, goes<br/>
      down into darkness<br/>
         fearlessly,<br/>
            she<br/>
   crawls on vertical walls,<br/>
  headed straight to the queen,<br/>
 bequeathing her strenuous load<br/>
  she is touched by strangers'<br/>
     antennae as she makes<br/>
        her way down,<br/>
          looking<br/>
            up<br/>
           only<br/>
         once, she<br/>
       arrives at the<br/>
    single fertile sister<br/>
     she envies lovingly<br/>
       and will serve<br/>
         ‘till she<br/>
            dies<br/><br/>
</div>
