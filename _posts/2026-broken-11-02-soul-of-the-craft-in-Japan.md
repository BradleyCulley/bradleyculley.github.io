<p>
Japanese cheesemakers in Hokkaido, such as "Niseko Cheese Factory", consistently rank among the best in the world
Japanese pizzas are also ranked among the best in the world
For example, The Pizza Bar on 38th (ranked 2nd globally in 2025<sup>1</sup>) and Pizza Marumo (ranked 3rd globally in 2026)
</p>

<p>
The core technology that will probably cure aging, the "Yamanaka factors", was discovered by Shinya Yamanaka and Kazutoshi Takahashi, while they were working at Kyoto University in Kyoto, Japan<sup>2</sup>.
It is the technology behind the first-ever FDA-cleared human clinical trial of age-reversal technology, which started in 2026<sup>3</sup>.
There are many more examples: physics, mathematics, literature, art, etc.
</p>

<p>
The Japanese idea of Kaizen—"continuous improvement"—might be at least part of the reason.
The "continuous improvement" phrasing doesn't do it justice, though. 
"Continuous improvement" evokes reducing error rates in assembly lines: useful but not deep.
</p>

<p>
I prefer the phrase "craftsperson mentality".
Dedicating one's life to honing a craft, no matter how good you get.
The honing itself becomes a goal and source of satisfaction; a fulfillment of the soul through fulfillment of human potential.
What an awesome idea. I hope to visit Japan someday.
</p>
