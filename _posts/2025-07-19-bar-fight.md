### Bar Fight

<br/>

<p>I'm 36 years old and I've never gotten into a bar fight in which someone smashed a beer bottle over my head.</p>

<p>I've <i>also</i> never smashed a beer bottle over someone else's head during a bar fight.</p>

<p>I've never had someone at a bar disrespect the woman I was there with, more or less obligating me to fight the individual in question.</p>

<p>I've never accidentally bumped into someone at a bar, had them refuse to accept my apology, and had the whole situation escalate until I was more or less obligated to fight the individual in question.</p>

<p>I've never had someone spill their drink on me (or my female partner) at a bar, making me more or less obligated to fight the drink-spiller.</p>

<p>All these situations I've avoided with a single technique: not going out drinking at bars.</p>
